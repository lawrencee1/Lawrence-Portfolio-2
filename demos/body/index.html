How it works

  1. Spin the body. Drag it around to find the spot. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  2. Tap where it hurts. The spot lights up.
  3. Follow along. Read the tips, then copy the short movement clips at your own pace.

Before you start

This is general information for everyday aches. It is not medical advice and cannot diagnose anything. Every area lists the warning signs that mean you should see a doctor or physiotherapist instead.

Move gently. A mild stretch is fine. Stop if pain turns sharp or spreads.
