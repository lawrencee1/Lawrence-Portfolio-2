Questions

Good to know before you come.

Will it hurt?

We numb the area fully and wait until it works. You can raise a hand at any time and we stop.

Do I pay before treatment?

No. You pay after each visit by card, QRIS, transfer or cash. Larger plans can be split across visits.

Can I use travel or health insurance?

We give you an itemised receipt and treatment report in English for your insurer to reimburse.

I'm only in Bali for a week. Is that enough?

For cleaning, whitening, fillings and most crowns, yes. Tell us your dates and we plan around them.

Where do I park?

There is free parking for cars and scooters in front of the studio.
