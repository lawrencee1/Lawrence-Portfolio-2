07 Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to film anything?

No. I build your content from maps, motion graphics, 3D, licensed clips and the photos you already have.

Can I really edit the website myself?

Yes. You log in, click the text or photo you want to change, and save. If you'd rather not, send me the change and I'll do it.

Do I pay a monthly fee to keep the website?

No. The website and its editor are a one-time price. You make changes yourself, so there is no maintenance fee.

Which businesses do you work with?

Clinics, gyms and wellness studios, and travel, hospitality and property brands.

What do you need from me?

A short call, your logo and photos if you have them, and a quick yes or no on each month's plan.

Do you work with businesses outside Bali?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Bali and Australia. Calls fit Australian business hours.
